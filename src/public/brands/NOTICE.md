# Provider marks

Each provider indicator bundles that provider’s mark: the OpenAI mark for Codex, the Claude mark, the Cursor mark, the GitHub Copilot mark for Copilot CLI, the OpenCode mark, the Google Gemini mark for Gemini CLI and the Google Antigravity mark. The SVG geometry is sourced from the corresponding Simple Icons/Wikimedia Commons brand entries (Antigravity: the outline of the product’s public favicon, without its colour fill), and is bundled only to identify the provider represented by dashboard data. Provider names and marks remain trademarks of their respective owners.
