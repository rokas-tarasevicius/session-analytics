Evidence & coverage

Every number on the dashboard is a lower bound built from local evidence. This panel lists what the current selection can and cannot establish.

Coverage notes

    What the local history can tell you

    Coverage is sessions with evidence / selected sessions. Summary and activity-only sources cannot establish all metrics. Unavailable is shown as a dash, never as zero activity. Counts do not prove success or quality.

    ProviderSourceFidelitySessionsToken evidenceTool evidenceTime basis

    How to read these numbers

    • API-equivalent value is a list-price equivalent of observed tokens, not an invoice. Provider-reported value is kept separate.
    • Coverage badges such as 87/116 show how many selected sessions carry the evidence behind a number; hover one for the sentence.
    • Ratios with tiny denominators are withheld; a dash means not applicable, never zero.
    • Activity counters do not prove success, quality, or value delivered.
